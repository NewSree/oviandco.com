The Problem

Three gaps. One patient pays for all of them.

Gap 01

Unseen interactions

Patients take AYUSH and modern medicines together and tell neither doctor. Herb-drug interactions go unnoticed.

Gap 02

Academy-to-practice gap

Low pay drives bright students out. Most graduates struggle with thousands of shlokas, get no placement, and fall back on symptom relief.

Gap 03

The solo clinic trap

Doctors run finance, HR, and marketing alone. Prime sites cost too much. Small-town clinics sit empty while skilled youth leave for jobs abroad.

The root: AYUSH care has no shared system for knowledge, software, or infrastructure.

Philosophy & Brand Story

Seeing the whole patient. Seeing the whole elephant.

Why Integrative Medicine

Structuring AYUSH. Partnering with modern medicine.

AYUSH streams today are neither structured nor organised as a unified clinical system. That lack of structure is why they are frequently criticised and dismissed as parallel disciplines—even though their therapeutic possibilities are deeply impressive to anyone who genuinely understands their clinical grace. Most people remain unaware of what authentic AYUSH care can offer, and one-sided ill-health campaigns further keep them from seeing the truth.

We do not oppose modern medicine. Modern medicine is the finest emergency management system humankind has ever built. Our mission is to popularise true integrative medicine—where doctors from every stream understand each other's possibilities and limitations, and join hands to see the whole picture of the patient, help them recover from illness, and restore lasting well-being.

Trained Workforce Pipeline

Hybrid Skilling & Compulsory Clinical Internship

Five 9-month (1,305-hour) allied vocational certification programmes have been submitted to ASAP Kerala—combining daily digital lessons, weekly district audio-visual sessions, and supervised full-time hospital and clinic internships.

Phase 1 · Months 1–3 (Weeks 1–12)

Daily Modules & District Orientation

  • Daily lessons: 20-minute chapter modules released Monday to Friday across 12 weeks.
  • Weekly audio-visual classes: Held at a district centre from 10:30 AM to 3:00 PM (12:30 PM – 1:00 PM lunch break).
  • Monthly workshops & add-ons: Monthly external-faculty workshops, plus optional hybrid (online + offline) add-on courses in Language Development, Grooming, Beauty Care, Kalari Uzhichil, and more.
  • Learning kit: Printed training manual and branded uniform provided for every student.
Phase 2 · From Week 13 Monday

Compulsory Internship, Assessment & Placement

  • Hospital & clinic placement: Initial batches intern at partnered hospitals until Ovi clinics are fully operational—8-hour daily shift (flexible to host hospital or Ovi clinic shift timings), with a weekly off plus 1 additional off per month.
  • Student support: Food and accommodation are handled by the host clinic.
  • Attendance & evaluation: Minimum 80% attendance across contact classes and internship; weekly and end-of-term assignments, internal evaluation, viva voce, and online examination.
  • Results & hiring: Results published within 2 weeks after internship; certified graduates can apply directly for Ovi clinic vacancies on this website.
